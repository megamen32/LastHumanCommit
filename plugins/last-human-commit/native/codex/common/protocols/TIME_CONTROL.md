# Time and progress

Ordinary work has no mandatory leaf clock, reporting interval or learning gate. Long-running work can opt into a quiet reminder. Use it to notice a real delay or change of direction, then continue the task.

Keep measured effort, elapsed time, estimates and external waits distinct. Preserve the original estimate when updating a forecast. A saved plan may use the existing timing fields; they are not a condition for making an ordinary source change.

Boss audits are mandatory. OpenCode 2's separate native Boss reviews observed active work about every 30 minutes, regardless of the general verbose/strict workflow flags. Only the user's direct instruction can suspend these audits. Its full prose is delivered to the parent session. That mechanism is distinct from the opt-in general workflow reminders.

`LHC_VERBOSE_WORKFLOW` enables the existing detailed reminders and `LHC_STRICT_WORKFLOW` selects the existing strict mode. Leave both off for normal outcome-first work. A reminder or missing telemetry is not a product result.
