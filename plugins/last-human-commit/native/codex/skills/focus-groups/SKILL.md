# Focus groups

Run a group when different users or devices could reveal different problems, or when the user asks for one. Each participant is a fresh Tester using the actual product. Simulated personas provide exploratory feedback, not evidence from real customers.

Prepare the candidate, test access and data in the agreed environment. Reuse the project's QA accounts and keep their protected references in `.agents/TEST_ACCESS.md`; clean transient test data when appropriate. Choose participant models through `model-availability`.

Give each tester a realistic goal and relevant context: experience, device and conditions. Choose variation that matters for this product. Keep the implementation and the expected verdict out of a first-contact test.

Let testers pursue their goals and report freely. Ask about a control when its meaning is unclear or matters to the result, rather than quizzing them on every button. Keep concrete observations, positive and negative, with screenshots of decisive moments.

The Lead separates defects, usability obstacles and preferences. Fix issues blocking the requested result, redeploy and retest the affected journeys. Run another fresh group when it will answer a remaining usability question. Stop when the intended goals work and no material uncertainty remains for this release.

Report the journeys tried, important feedback, repairs and remaining findings.
