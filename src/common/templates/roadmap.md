## Backlog

Everything wanted but not prioritized. One line per item: `- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD <what> (<source>)`.
The date is the deadline, by default the day it was added + 2 weeks. Overdue lines are taken into the
current full cycle without a reminder (`rg -n "^- \[ \] 20" ROADMAP.md`). A line leaves done, or deleted
on the owner's word; its date is never just moved.
