# Make LHC business-first and least-cost

## Raw business request

The user supplied multiple retrospectives where LHC process displaced the
business result: mandatory orchestration and audits, premature strict proof,
micro-review loops, task-card confusion, late tracing of the production path,
estimate extensions instead of route changes, and incorrect waiting for active
Workers. The decisive request is:

> Сначала подумай что их всех связывает долго и внимательно! выведи мне очень
> длинный список ошибок. потом без сабагентов и плагинов а чисто прочтением всех
> инструкций last human commit загрузи себе в контекст(прочти) и измени чтобы
> БИЗНЕС логика была строго первична, least cost agent.

The user additionally corrected the Worker control contract: use a real wait
tool rather than ending while a Worker is active; 20 minutes is a reporting and
management checkpoint, not a kill deadline; Lead should ask Overseer whether
the route remains sound or redirect the Worker to the shortest route; killing
agents is exceptional.

## Outcome

The authoritative Last Human Commit instructions make the next real business
result the primary optimization target and select the least-cost sufficient
agent, route, proof, and governance. Process artifacts and role gates cannot
become prerequisites unless proportional to a concrete risk or observed
failure.

## Exact business canary

Source validation must reject the old unconditional behavior and prove all of
these normative contracts across the authoritative instruction surface:

1. business value and the shortest real user canary are decided before role or
   process routing;
2. Lead may directly research and implement when delegation costs more than the
   next business proof, regardless of a five-minute boundary;
3. Overseer, Reviewer, Critic, Adviser, and Tester are risk/event-triggered,
   not mandatory for every task or every wave;
4. a 20-minute Worker limit is a checkpoint requiring a progress/business-
   delta report and route decision, not cancellation or forced replacement;
5. active Workers are joined through the harness wait mechanism; Lead does not
   finish merely because no terminal result has arrived;
6. MVP acceptance uses the cheapest proof sufficient for the user's present
   claim; stricter proof and hardening are deferred until requested or observed
   as necessary;
7. task records remain compact control surfaces and do not force snapshot
   commits or append-only histories that cost more than the work;
8. existing workspace safety and explicit consequential-action boundaries are
   preserved.
